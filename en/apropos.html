About

Why this site

I made this site to gather my ideas and projects in one place: download them, see how to use them and write to me if there is a problem.

Why TaskbarNest

What annoyed me in Windows was not being able to create folders to organize my taskbar better. I had more than 20 icons in it.

What you will find here

For now TaskbarNest, app folders in the Windows 11 taskbar.

More programs will come. They will be free. Maybe some will be paid later, but I do not think so.

What is next

Later, I would like to start another project: a program to scan your computer, a bit like TreeSize, mixed with CCleaner to clean up files. It will also tell exactly where a file comes from, because sometimes that is not very detailed.

Who I am

I have always wanted to create games and software without learning to code. So AI writes the code.

I am being honest about it: it is not the usual way, but it lets me build ideas I could never have coded on my own.

Support me
